Rule 1. Short title, and Commencement
(Source page(s): [1])
These rules may be called the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011. They come into force on 1 April 2011.

------------------------------------------------------------

Rule 2. Definitions
(Source page(s): [1])
This rule defines terms used in the rules, including dealer, lot, manufacturer, maximum permissible error, net quantity, packer, principal display panel, quantity, retail dealer, package, retail sale, retail sale price, section, schedule, standard package, wholesale dealer and wholesale package.

------------------------------------------------------------

Rule 3. Applicability of the Chapter
(Source page(s): [1])
This rule specifies the applicability of the provisions of the chapter and the packages or commodities to which the provisions apply or do not apply.

------------------------------------------------------------

Rule 4. Pre-packing or import for sale
(Source page(s): [1])
This rule deals with requirements relating to pre-packing or importing commodities for sale, distribution or delivery.

------------------------------------------------------------

Rule 5. Specific commodities to be packed and sold in recommended standard
(Source page(s): [1])
packages This rule deals with specified commodities that are required to be packed and sold in recommended standard quantities/packages.

------------------------------------------------------------

Rule 6. Declarations to be made on every package
(Source page(s): [1])
Every package is required to bear the prescribed declarations. These include applicable declarations relating to the manufacturer/packer/importer, commodity, net quantity, retail sale price and consumer-related information, subject to the specific requirements and exemptions in the rules.

------------------------------------------------------------

Rule 7. Principal display panel — its area, size and letter etc.
(Source page(s): [1])
This rule specifies requirements concerning the principal display panel, including its area, size and the manner in which prescribed declarations are displayed.

------------------------------------------------------------

Rule 8. Declaration where to appear
(Source page(s): [1])
This rule specifies where the required declarations are to appear on the package.

------------------------------------------------------------

Rule 9. Manner in which declaration shall be made
(Source page(s): [1])
This rule specifies the manner in which declarations must be made, including requirements concerning visibility, legibility and the prescribed presentation of declarations.

------------------------------------------------------------

Rule 10. Declaration of name and address of the manufacturer, etc.
(Source page(s): [1])
This rule specifies requirements for declaring the name and address of the manufacturer, packer and/or importer, as applicable.

------------------------------------------------------------

Rule 11. General provisions relating to declaration of quantity
(Source page(s): [2])
This rule contains general requirements for declaring the quantity of the commodity in the package.

------------------------------------------------------------

Rule 12. Manner in which declaration of quantity shall be made
(Source page(s): [2])
This rule specifies the manner and form in which the quantity declaration is to be made.

------------------------------------------------------------

Rule 13. Statement of units of weight, measure or number
(Source page(s): [2])
This rule specifies the units in which declarations of weight, measure or number are to be expressed.

------------------------------------------------------------

Rule 14. Declarations with regard to dimensions of certain commodities
(Source page(s): [2])
This rule contains requirements for declarations concerning dimensions of specified commodities.

------------------------------------------------------------

Rule 15. Declaration with regard to dimensions and weight to be made on
(Source page(s): [2])
packages This rule specifies declarations concerning dimensions and weight for applicable packages.

------------------------------------------------------------

Rule 16. Declarations to be made with regard to the number of usable units
(Source page(s): [2])
This rule deals with declarations concerning the number of usable units where applicable.

------------------------------------------------------------

Rule 17. Declarations with regard to the dimensions of container type packages
(Source page(s): [2])
This rule specifies declaration requirements concerning dimensions for container-type packages.

------------------------------------------------------------

Rule 18. Provisions relating to wholesale dealer and retail dealers
(Source page(s): [2])
This rule specifies obligations and restrictions applicable to wholesale dealers and retail dealers in relation to packaged commodities.

------------------------------------------------------------

Rule 19. Inspection of quantity and error in packages at the premises of the
(Source page(s): [2])
manufacturer/packer/importer This rule deals with inspection of packages and verification of quantity and permissible error at the relevant premises.

------------------------------------------------------------

Rule 20. Action to be taken on completion of inspection of packages
(Source page(s): [2])
This rule specifies action to be taken after inspection of packages.

------------------------------------------------------------

Rule 21. Inspection of quantity and error in packages at the premises of wholesale
(Source page(s): [2])
dealer This rule deals with inspection and verification of quantity and error in packages at wholesale dealer premises.

------------------------------------------------------------

Rule 22. Establishment of maximum permissible error on package
(Source page(s): [2])
This rule deals with the establishment/application of maximum permissible error for packages.

------------------------------------------------------------

Rule 23. Deceptive packages to be repacked or in default to be seized
(Source page(s): [2])
This rule deals with packages considered deceptive and provides for repacking or seizure in the circumstances specified by the rules.

------------------------------------------------------------

Rule 24. Wholesale package
(Source page(s): [2])
This rule specifies declarations and other requirements applicable to wholesale packages.

------------------------------------------------------------

Rule 25. Restrictions on sale of export packages in India
(Source page(s): [2])
This rule places restrictions on the sale of export packages in India, subject to the conditions specified in the rules.

------------------------------------------------------------

Rule 26. Exemption in respect of certain packages
(Source page(s): [3])
This rule provides exemptions for specified packages/categories, subject to the conditions stated in the rules.

------------------------------------------------------------

Rule 27. Registration of manufacturers, packers and importers
(Source page(s): [3])
This rule contains requirements relating to registration of manufacturers, packers and importers.

------------------------------------------------------------

Rule 28. Registration of shorter address permissible
(Source page(s): [3])
This rule provides for circumstances in which a shorter address may be used for registration/declaration, subject to the prescribed requirements.

------------------------------------------------------------

Rule 29. Registration of manufacturers and packers, etc.
(Source page(s): [3])
This rule contains further provisions concerning registration of manufacturers, packers and related persons.

------------------------------------------------------------

Rule 30. Compilation of lists of manufacturers or packers and their circulation
(Source page(s): [3])
This rule deals with compilation and circulation of lists of manufacturers or packers.

------------------------------------------------------------

Rule 31. Prohibition of advertisements
(Source page(s): [3])
This rule contains requirements/restrictions relating to advertisements and declarations concerning packaged commodities.

------------------------------------------------------------

Rule 32. Penalty for contravention of Rules
(Source page(s): [3])
This rule deals with consequences/penalties for contravention of the rules.

------------------------------------------------------------

Rule 33. Power to relax
(Source page(s): [3])
This rule provides the Central Government with power to relax specified requirements in the circumstances and manner provided by the rules.

------------------------------------------------------------

Rule 34. Repeal and savings
(Source page(s): [3])
This rule deals with repeal and savings, including the treatment of the earlier Standards of Weights and Measures (Packaged Commodities) Rules and matters saved by the new rules.
